## 💡 THE BIG INSIGHT

**Everything in DSA is a consequence of three facts:**

1. **Memory is finite** 
   - Recursion depth is limited
   - Caches exist for performance
   - Allocation is slow

2. **Operations take time**
   - Different algorithms scale differently
   - O(n²) becomes infeasible at scale
   - Constants matter at small scale

3. **Everything trades off**
   - Time vs. Space (Memoization)
   - Simplicity vs. Performance
   - Predictability vs. Speed

**Master these three, and you can learn any algorithm by analyzing its trade-offs.**
