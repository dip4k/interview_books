# Week 5: Roadmap & Time Budget

**Week:** 5 | **Total Time:** 15-20 hours | **Start:** [Date] | **End:** [Date]

---

## 📅 WEEKLY MISSION

Master hierarchical data structures (trees) to enable O(log n) algorithms on complex data.  
From theory (anatomy) → applications (BSTs, heaps, balancing) → production awareness (databases).

---

## ⏱️ TIME ALLOCATION (HOUR-BY-HOUR)

### Phase 1: Foundations (Days 1-2) - 5 hours total

**Day 1: Binary Tree Anatomy** (2.5 hours)
- Reading: 70 min (THE WHY through RETENTION HOOK)
- Hands-on: 40 min (build trees, implement basics)
- Practice: 30 min (solve property problems)

**Day 2: Tree Traversals** (2.5 hours)
- Reading: 75 min (all 11 sections)
- Hands-on: 45 min (implement 4 traversals)
- Practice: 30 min (trace by hand, verify outputs)

### Phase 2: Application (Days 3-4) - 5.5 hours total

**Day 3: Binary Search Trees** (2.75 hours)
- Reading: 80 min
- Hands-on: 50 min (search/insert/delete implementation)
- Practice: 45 min (solve BST problems)

**Day 4: Heaps & Priority Queues** (2.75 hours)
- Reading: 75 min
- Hands-on: 45 min (implement heap operations)
- Practice: 45 min (solve heap/priority queue problems)

### Phase 3: Advanced (Day 5) - 3 hours

**Day 5: Balanced Trees** (3 hours)
- Reading: 90 min (most complex topic)
- Hands-on: 60 min (understand rotations)
- Practice: 30 min (trace rebalancing)

### Phase 4: Integration & Review (1-4 hours)

**Integration Day(s):**
- Solve 15+ mixed tree problems
- Connect concepts across days
- Practice interviews
- Build project using trees

- Do combined review day before moving to next week

### Behind by 3+ hours?
- Extend week 5 to 1.5 weeks
- Focus on Days 1-4 (core), compress Day 5
- Return to Day 5 before Week 5.5 content

- Rewatch visualization videos
